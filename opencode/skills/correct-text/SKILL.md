# Correct Text

When asked to correct text, apply these rules:

## What to do

- Fix grammar errors: tense, subject-verb agreement, punctuation, capitalization, and spelling.
- Fix writing problems: run-on sentences, awkward phrasing, unclear pronoun references, missing articles, and word repetition.
- Improve sentence flow where the meaning is preserved but the phrasing is clunky.
- Preserve all ideas, opinions, examples, and anecdotes intact. Every paragraph and sentence should map back to the original.
- Preserve the original style, tone, and voice.
- Keep headings, links, and formatting intact.

## What NOT to do

- Do not simplify, shorten, or condense the text. Keep the same level of detail.
- Do not merge or split paragraphs unless it's an obvious formatting error.
- Do not use dashes (em dash `—` or en dash `–`). Use commas instead.
- Do not add new ideas, examples, or opinions.
- Do not change the meaning or introduce new opinions.
- Do not remove disclaimers or nuance that the author intentionally included.

## Output

Return the corrected text and update the original file with the changes. Do not include preamble, explanation, or commentary.
