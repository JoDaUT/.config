# Simplify Text

When asked to simplify text, apply these rules:

## What to do

- Merge related sentences into tighter paragraphs.
- Cut filler phrases like "of course," "to be honest," "I think," "it's worth noting."
- Remove redundant explanations — say it once, clearly.
- Shorten anecdotes to their essential point.
- Combine adjacent paragraphs that serve the same idea.
- Preserve the original style, tone, voice, and all key messages.
- Keep headings, links, and formatting intact.

## What NOT to do

- Do not use dashes (em dash `—` or en dash `–`). Use commas instead.
- Do not add new ideas or expand on the author's points.
- Do not change the meaning or introduce new opinions.
- Do not remove disclaimers or nuance that the author intentionally included.

## Output

Return the simplified text and update the original file with the changes. Do not include preamble, explanation, or commentary.
